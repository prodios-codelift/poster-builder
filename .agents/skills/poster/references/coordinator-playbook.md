# Social media coordinator playbook

Think in this order: **who is scrolling → what should they feel → what one thing should they do → what makes them stop.** Design comes after.

## 1. Goal → archetype → CTA

| Goal | Strong archetypes | CTA examples | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Awareness | Big-type statement, hero photo | "Follow for more on …" | Reach, impressions |
| Engagement | Question in big type, before/after, hot take | "Which one are you? Comment A or B" | Comments, shares |
| Click / sign-up | Announcement, tip card with a payoff | "Get the guide — link in comments" | Clicks, CTR |
| Event attendance | Event/date card, hero photo of the venue or city | "Save your seat — 12 Nov, 4 pm GST" | Registrations |
| Hiring | Announcement + role + 3 perks (icon rows) | "Apply — link in comments" | Applicants |
| Launch / news | Announcement, product-free hero + bold claim | "Try it free" | Sign-ups, mentions |
| Social proof | Stat card or quote card (**real** data only) | "See the case study" | Saves, clicks |

One goal per poster. If the user wants two (for example, "announce the launch and hire"), suggest two posts.

## 2. The thumb-stop test

A feed shows the poster at about 300–400 px wide for about 1.5 seconds. Check:

1. **Squint test**: blur your eyes. Is there one obvious focal point?
2. **3-second read**: headline + focal point alone carry the message.
3. **Contrast**: text ≥ 4.5:1 against its background, big headlines ≥ 3:1. It should also work in grayscale.
4. **Pattern break**: does it look different from the last 5 posts in this feed (colour, shape, scale)? Big type, a bold colour field or an unexpected crop beats another blue gradient.
5. **Sound-off, caption-off**: the poster makes sense without the caption.

## 3. Hook formulas (headline)

Keep to ≤ 7 words, concrete, and in the reader's language. Pick the formula that fits the goal:

- **Contrarian**: "Stop posting every day."
- **Specific promise**: "Close deals faster on WhatsApp."
- **Question**: "Still answering leads by email?"
- **Number** (real only): "3 inbox habits that cost deals."
- **Name the pain**: "Five apps. One customer. Zero context."
- **Before → after**: "From 5 inboxes to 1."
- **Time / urgency** (events): "Tomorrow, 4 pm: AI in sales."
- **Identity**: "For founders hiring engineer #1."

Avoid vague hype ("Revolutionise your workflow!"), jargon, all caps longer than about 4 words, and exclamation marks.

## 4. Brand consistency

- Use the brand's palette and theme every time. Recognition comes from repetition. If a theme fits the brand, stick with it across posts.
- One display face + one body face. One accent colour, used sparingly.
- Logo or handle in the same corner on every post. Small, never the focal point.
- Keep the tone consistent with the brand voice (formal B2B vs playful consumer).

## 5. Accessibility and inclusion

- Contrast as in the thumb-stop test. Don't rely on colour alone (use icons or labels for do/don't).
- Text ≥ 28 px on the 1080 canvas.
- Write **alt text** for every poster.
- Inclusive, region-appropriate imagery (see `imagery.md`). Avoid stereotypes.
- Plain language: short words, active voice, no idioms that don't translate (posts in the Gulf, India and the EU are read by second-language English speakers).

## 6. Deliverables at hand-off

### Caption template

```
<Hook line, which can echo the poster headline but shouldn't repeat it word for word>

<2–4 short lines of value: why it matters, what they get, key details>

<CTA: one action, with the link or "link in comments">

<3–5 hashtags>
```

- **LinkedIn**: the first ~140 characters show before "…see more", so put the hook there. Links in the post body can reduce reach, so many coordinators put the link in the first comment. Offer both. 3–5 hashtags, specific over generic (`#B2BSales` over `#business`).
- **Instagram**: links aren't clickable in captions ("link in bio" or a story link sticker). 3–8 hashtags, mixing niche and mid-size.
- **X**: ≤ 280 characters total, 1–2 hashtags at most, link inline.

### Alt text template

`<What it is>: <main visual>. Text reads "<headline>" <and key details>.`, ≤ 125 characters.
Example: `Event poster: Dubai skyline at dusk. Text reads "AI in sales — live, 12 Nov, 4 pm GST".`

### First comment (optional)

The link plus one line of context: "Register here → <link>. Seats are limited to 100."

## 7. Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] One goal, one message, one CTA.
- [ ] Headline ≤ 7 words, total ≤ ~25 words on the poster.
- [ ] All facts, numbers, quotes, dates, prices and names come from the user. Nothing invented.
- [ ] Event details are complete: date, time **with time zone**, place or "online", link.
- [ ] Spelling of names, brands and handles double-checked.
- [ ] Photo is credited in `assets/CREDITS.md`, under the free Unsplash License, and not presented as evidence.
- [ ] Icons are from one family and one style, coloured with tokens, and Solar is credited if used.
- [ ] Passes the thumb-stop test at thumbnail size.
- [ ] Caption, hashtags and alt text written.
